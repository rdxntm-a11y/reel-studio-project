CHANGES MADE
1. Beat-local camera timing and motion
   - src/reels/lead-masks/style/types.ts: SceneProps now includes `frames`.
   - src/reels/lead-masks/Reel.tsx: each Beat passes its real beat length to the scene.
   - All 8 scene files: the final camera key now uses `frames`, not the full composition duration; Scene06's push interpolations also use `frames`.
   - src/reels/lead-masks/style/motion.ts: camera() now uses deterministic Easing.inOut(Easing.cubic) with clamped extrapolation, so every pan/zoom reaches its intended pose exactly at the beat boundary.
   - Removed the unused, full-duration CameraDrift helper from style/components/ui.tsx.
2. Typography and layout
   - src/reels/lead-masks/style/theme.ts: ZONE now records the real measured zones (headline 220/264, hero 484/792, caption 1310/190).
   - src/reels/lead-masks/style/components/SceneLayout.tsx: uses ZONE instead of duplicated padding numbers and renders the shared subtle paper background.
   - src/reels/lead-masks/style/components/Headline.tsx: removed the flat Math.min(size, 96) cap. Font size is now computed from requested size, container width, line length, line count and the real headline height; effective sizes are roughly 105-126px. Added a 3-frame stagger between lines and kept a clear label -> headline -> caption hierarchy.
3. Visual consistency and motion restraint
   - src/reels/lead-masks/style/components/PaperBackground.tsx: added an optional `subtle` mode (lighter grain, folds and vignette) and a seed-derived filter id so multiple instances cannot collide.
   - SceneLayout uses subtle paper on every story beat; the CTA keeps the stronger original treatment.
   - ScreenShake was reduced from 10 beats to 4 deliberate punches: Scene01 (1966), Scene04Discovery (3 days later / bodies), Scene07Unsolved (UNSOLVED), Scene08Theory (OVERDOSE).
4. Image framing
   - Kept objectFit:"contain" because all source plates are opaque 2:3 illustrations and cropping would remove story information.
   - The corrected camera moves now actually zoom to their authored end poses (roughly 1.10-1.26 scale), which reduces the paper gutters and makes the evidence plates fill the hero area without cropping content.
   - Existing PropLabel/Tag/Stamp annotations remain in the side gutters, so the negative space is used as dossier margin rather than empty space.
5. Captions
   - src/reels/lead-masks/style/components/Caption.tsx: dark ink capsule with a paper-light hairline border, cream spoken words and the existing yellow active-word highlight. Chunking, timing, word order, font family and sizing were left unchanged.
