WHAT WAS ENHANCED

1. Shared motion language
- src/reels/lead-masks/style/motion.ts
  - Added `progress()` for one-shot decelerating reveals.
  - Extended `ease()` with an optional easing curve (default cubic-out).
  - Added `p01()` and `parallax()` helpers.
  - Kept all camera moves deterministic and clamped. Camera keys always end on the local beat length (`frames`), never the full composition.
- Result: slower, controlled push-ins and lateral drifts instead of repeated generic zooms. Camera scale stays in a restrained ~1.00-1.16 range.

2. Headline system
- src/reels/lead-masks/style/components/Headline.tsx
  - Added six distinct entrance variants: rise, mask, track, split, press, dateline.
  - Added an optional editorial rule beside the kicker, with a timeline node for dateline beats.
  - Preserved the existing type sizing, colour hierarchy and uppercase treatment.
  - Consecutive beats no longer share the same headline entrance.

3. New visual primitives
- src/reels/lead-masks/style/components/ui.tsx
  - `Rule`: a hairline that draws in from a word anchor, horizontal or vertical.
  - `EvidencePlate`: a cream mat, ink border and paper shadow around an evidence image. The camera move happens inside the plate so zooms crop inside the mount rather than spilling into the dossier margins.
  - `AtmosphereGlow`: a low-opacity, breathing radial glow reserved for the signal/sky beat.
- `ScreenShake` remains available but is used only as a deliberate punctuation, not as an entrance default.

4. Beat-by-beat changes
- Scene01 (Brazil 1966): controlled establishing push-in, track headline with accent rule, foreground label parallax, staggered clue labels.
- Scene02 (Manoel + Miguel): lateral two-shot drift, split headline, labels enter from opposite edges.
- Scene03Clock (August 17): slow push, dateline headline with red rule and timeline node.
- Scene03Receipt (Nervous waiting): waitress-to-Miguel pan preserved; reduced final scale to 1.16; masked headline; the watch reveal is now a cream witness card with a red top rule that drops and rotates into place.
- Scene04Discovery (Three days later): preserved the down-tilt from the kite to the bodies; added a 17 AUG -> 20 AUG time spine in the headline band with a travelling marker.
- Scene04Bodies (The outfit): image mounted as an evidence plate; press headline with accent rule; LEAD MASK stamp moved to the lower-right so it does not cover the suit.
- Scene05 (No wounds / no fight): slow push, vertical forensic spine beside the three clues drawn on the first clue word, labels shifted right to sit on the spine.
- Scene06Notebook (The notebook): document clipped open with a scan line on the first ~16 frames, then the torn-paper instruction is laid on top.
- Scene06Capsules (Take the capsules): evidence plate entrance, slow push, split headline, torn note from the right, staggered two-capsule pop.
- Scene06Metal (Wait for the signal): slow push, slower signal rings, soft breathing glow behind the mask, rise headline with rule, torn notes layered flat.
- Scene07Autopsy (Too late): slow push, press headline with rule, LATE stamp on the word, decomposed-organs label on its own word. No shake.
- Scene07Unsolved (No answer): masked headline with rule, three question marks tied to the three spoken phrases with slight depth drift, UNSOLVED stamp on the final phrase.
- Scene08Theory (Signal or overdose): slow tilt-up camera, atmospheric glow on the UFO word, split headline, THEORY and OVERDOSE stamps on their words, one small screen shake on OVERDOSE.
- Scene08CTA (End card): eased entrance replaces the spring pop; kicker, two title lines and handle stagger in; FOLLOW stamp lands on the spoken Follow word; slow push/hold with no fade-out.

5. Safety and probe fixes
- Dateline rule node moved inside its own box so the layout probe no longer sees a 7 px overflow.
- Signal pulse reduced from 260 px to 208 px so it stays inside the 220 px mask box and cannot trip the hero overflow check.
- CTA text stagger starts immediately enough that the end-card is never visible with no readable text at sampled frames.
