6. ANIMATION AND MOTION ANALYSIS
======================================================================

Motion primitives (verified)
- style/motion.ts:8-9 springAt: delayed spring (defined, not used by scenes).
- style/motion.ts:15-23 slamPose: 3-frame 2->1 scale slam, damped sine settle, 9-frame sideways shake. Used by Stamp.tsx:29.
- style/motion.ts:30-39 camera: linear interpolate of x/y/scale across sorted keyframes, then CSS translate+scale transform. No easing curve; linear between keys.
- style/motion.ts:42-43 ease: generic interpolate wrapper (defined, not used by scenes).
- style/motion.ts:46-52 slide: directional translate for spring progress; used by PropLabel, TornNote, CapsuleRow, TubeReveal.
- theme.ts:49-53 SPRING configs: slam (damping 12/stiffness 220/mass .7), pop (13/180/.6), drop (9/160/.9).
- ui.tsx:11-28 ScreenShake: sine/cosine decay over 16 frames, default amplitude 2.4 px; applied to 9 of the 14 beats plus the CTA (10 total), with the CTA overriding amount to 1.8.
- ui.tsx:30-37 Floaty and :39-60 CameraDrift are defined but never used.
- PaperBackground.tsx uses static SVG feTurbulence grain plus radial light and inset vignette; only Scene08CTA uses it.

Verified systemic defect: composition duration used as scene-local key.
Scenes calling useVideoConfig().durationInFrames and passing it as a local camera/push key: Scene01:25, Scene02:23, Scene03:22 and :59, Scene04:28 and :82, Scene05:25, Scene06:69/:92/:157 (push), Scene07:22 and :95, Scene08:27. useVideoConfig() returns the composition duration (2327), not the Sequence duration. Result: the final camera pose is almost never reached and the Scene06 image push is nearly zero. Concrete example: Scene01 camera has keys at local 0/58/130/2327 but the beat ends at 184, so only 2.5% of the last segment plays. This makes the camera work read as a very subtle drift rather than the intended reveal.

Animation quality assessment (verified from code)
- Positives: deterministic frame-driven motion, word-anchored delays, three distinct spring personalities, a strong stamp slam, a handwriting reveal in Scene06, and a real karaoke caption layer. All CSS used (oklch, color-mix, container queries, cqi, clip-path) is Chromium-deterministic and appropriate for Remotion.
- Negatives: camera interpolation is linear; repeated pop/slide/slam across nearly every beat; screen shake is used as a universal punctuation mark; question marks float on a free-running sine; signal rings are a free-running 24-frame loop; no transitions between beats (hard cuts only); no depth/parallax; no focus-pull or optical zoom language; no use of beat-synchronous cutting because no music track exists.

Visual consistency (verified): high internal consistency of the paper-and-red system, but Scene06's Caveat handwriting and Scene08CTA's grain/vignette are isolated one-offs. Headlines are nominally assigned 130-170 px in every scene, yet Headline.tsx:69 clamps every one to Math.min(size, 96), so the actual headline size is uniformly 96 px. That flattens the intended type hierarchy across all 13 headline beats.
