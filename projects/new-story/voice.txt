Ye keeda cockroach ko maarta nahi, use zombie bana deta hai. Doosra danc seedha dimaag ke ek khaas hisse mein. Zeher bhaagne ka signal hi band kar deta hai. Wasp cockroach ka antenna pakadkar use patte ki tarah le jaata hai. Aur phir uska bachcha use andar se khaata hai. Kudrat, sabse badi horror story hai.
