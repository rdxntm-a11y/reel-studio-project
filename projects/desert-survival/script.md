## Twist

His suicide attempt failed only because dehydration had made his blood too thick to drain. Waking alive became the turning point that kept him walking until the oasis and the nomad camp.

## Facts and sources

- Mauro Prosperi took part in the 1994 Marathon des Sables, a six-day, 250 km race through the Sahara, at age 39: https://www.bbc.com/news/magazine-30046426
- An eight-hour sandstorm hit on day four and he became lost: https://www.bbc.com/news/magazine-30046426 and https://www.theguardian.com/lifeandstyle/2023/jul/04/how-we-survive-mauro-prosperi-lost-desert-raw-bats-urine
- He had half a bottle of water left and urinated into a spare bottle, drinking it later: https://www.bbc.com/news/magazine-30046426
- He took shelter in a marabout (desert shrine), killed bats and drank their blood, eating at least 20 raw: https://www.bbc.com/news/magazine-30046426 and https://www.independent.co.uk/news/world/europe/marathon-runner-mauro-prosperi-drank-urine-and-ate-bats-snakes-and-lizards-to-survive-in-the-desert-for-over-a-week-9888424.html
- A rescue helicopter flew over but did not see him: https://www.bbc.com/news/magazine-30046426
- He cut his wrists, but dehydration had thickened his blood so it would not drain; he woke alive the next morning: https://www.bbc.com/news/magazine-30046426
- On day eight he found an oasis; the next day a shepherd girl and a Berber nomad camp helped him, and he learned he had crossed into Algeria: https://www.bbc.com/news/magazine-30046426
- He was 291 km off course, lost 16 kg (down to 45 kg), and took almost two years to recover: https://www.bbc.com/news/magazine-30046426 and https://www.abc.net.au/news/2022-01-01/marathon-des-sables-mauro-prosperi/100726892
