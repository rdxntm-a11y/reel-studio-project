Someone was hiding inside the White House network… and it didn’t look like a hack. Security researchers say they spotted residential proxy traffic coming from the White House network. Not a movie-style break-in. Not a blinking red alert. Something quieter. Residential proxies are the kind that make internet traffic look like it’s coming from a normal home. That’s what makes this creepy. If the traffic looks like a regular person on a regular Wi-Fi connection, it blends in. It can slip past the kind of suspicion you’d have for a strange server in another country. So the question isn’t just, “How did this happen?” It’s, “Who was using that cover, and what were they trying to reach?” We don’t have every answer yet. But when a network that powerful shows signs of hidden traffic, it’s a reminder: the scariest threats don’t always kick the door down. Sometimes they walk in wearing the mask of ordinary life.
